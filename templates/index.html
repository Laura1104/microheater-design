Why Microheater Design?

Designing a microheater should not start
with trial and error.

Microheater performance depends strongly on its geometry and operating conditions. Finding a suitable design can require repeated simulations, parameter adjustments, and testing.

Define

Define the microheater geometry.

Evaluate

Simulate or test its thermal response.

Compare

Check whether the target temperature is reached.

If the target is not reached, adjust the design and repeat.

Microheater Design approaches this problem in reverse.

Instead of starting with a geometry and asking what temperature it will produce, the system starts with the desired thermal response and predicts a suitable microheater design.
